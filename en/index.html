Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Mora counts your steps with your iPhone, using Health and Motion & Fitness. Nothing else to wear.

How does Mora motivate me to walk more?

Mora turns walking into a game: a step goal made for you, a streak of days to grow, a celebration every time you hit your goal and a coach who cheers you on in the tone you pick.

Do I need 10,000 steps a day?

Not necessarily. 10,000 is a benchmark, not a rule. Mora starts from your current average to suggest a realistic goal, which you can change anytime in your profile.

Does it work without opening the app?

Yes. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your steps and streak, and Mora sends you encouragement throughout the day.

Can I choose how Mora cheers me on?

Yes. Pick how Mora talks to you: Sports coach, Queen (or Boss) or Scientist. In your profile you also choose which messages you get (morning note, halfway, evening reminder…) and when.

Is Mora free?

7 days free, then €39.99 per year (€3.33 a month) or €7.99 per month, no commitment. Cancel anytime.

Is Mora on Android?

Not yet. Mora is coming to iPhone first; Android will follow.
